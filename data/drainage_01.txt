Proper drainage is essential for preventing waterlogging, which can damage plant roots and reduce crop yield.
Farmers install drainage systems to remove excess water from farmland after heavy rainfall or irrigation.
Poor drainage often leads to soil erosion, nutrient loss, and increased risk of plant disease.
Well-drained soil supports healthy root development and improves overall crop productivity.
Drainage management is particularly important for crops sensitive to excess moisture, such as tomatoes.
Farmers combine irrigation and drainage planning to maintain optimal soil moisture levels throughout the growing season.
Effective drainage systems protect farmland from long-term degradation caused by waterlogging and soil compaction.
