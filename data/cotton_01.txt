Cotton is an important cash crop grown primarily for its fiber, which is used in textile production.
Cotton cultivation requires warm climate, well-drained soil, and moderate rainfall for healthy growth.
Farmers use fertilizer containing nitrogen and potassium to support cotton plant growth and fiber quality.
Pests such as bollworms pose a major threat to cotton crops, requiring careful pesticide management.
Proper irrigation scheduling helps cotton plants develop strong roots and healthy fiber-producing bolls.
Weed management is essential in cotton fields to prevent competition for nutrients and sunlight.
Harvesting cotton at the right time ensures better fiber quality and higher market value.
