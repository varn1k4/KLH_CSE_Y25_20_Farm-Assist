Rice is one of the most important cereal crops cultivated in India. Rice requires sufficient water during its growing season. Farmers should monitor rice fields regularly for pests and diseases. Soil fertility plays an important role in rice production.

Wheat is another important cereal crop. Wheat grows well in fertile and well-drained soil. Proper irrigation improves wheat growth. Farmers should monitor wheat plants for fungal diseases and insects.

Tomato is an important vegetable crop. Tomato plants require sunlight, adequate water, and nutrient-rich soil. Farmers should regularly inspect tomato plants for pests and diseases.

Soil testing helps farmers determine the nutrient requirements of their crops. Soil moisture is important for healthy crop growth. Excessive irrigation can cause root diseases.

Pest management is important for protecting crops. Farmers should identify pests at an early stage. Integrated pest management can reduce crop damage while minimizing unnecessary pesticide use.

Drip irrigation supplies water directly to the root zone of plants. It can reduce water wastage and improve irrigation efficiency.

Weather information can help farmers plan sowing, irrigation, harvesting, and pest management activities. Rainfall and temperature influence crop growth and agricultural productivity.
