Irrigation is the artificial application of water to crops when natural rainfall is insufficient for healthy growth.
Farmers use various irrigation methods including flood irrigation, drip irrigation, and sprinkler systems.
Proper irrigation scheduling ensures that crops receive adequate water during critical growth stages.
Efficient water management through irrigation can significantly improve crop yield and reduce water wastage.
Drip irrigation delivers water directly to plant roots, minimizing evaporation and conserving water resources.
Over-irrigation can lead to waterlogging, root disease, and reduced soil fertility in farmland.
Climate conditions such as rainfall and temperature directly influence irrigation requirements for different crops.
