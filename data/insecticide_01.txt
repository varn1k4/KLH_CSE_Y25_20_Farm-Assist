Insecticides are chemical or biological substances used by farmers to control harmful insect pests in crops.
Proper insecticide application protects plant leaves, stems, and roots from significant pest damage.
Farmers must follow recommended dosages to avoid harming beneficial insects and contaminating soil or water.
Overuse of insecticide can lead to pest resistance, making future pest control more difficult.
Integrated pest management combines insecticide use with biological control methods for more sustainable results.
Timely insecticide application, based on regular field monitoring, helps minimize crop damage and yield loss.
Safe handling and storage of insecticide is essential for farmer health and environmental protection.
