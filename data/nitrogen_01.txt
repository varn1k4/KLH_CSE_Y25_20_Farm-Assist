Nitrogen is one of the most important nutrients required for healthy plant growth and crop productivity.
It plays a key role in leaf and stem development, giving plants their characteristic green color.
Farmers apply nitrogen-based fertilizer to support vigorous vegetative growth during early crop stages.
Nitrogen deficiency often causes yellowing leaves and stunted growth, reducing overall crop yield.
Excess nitrogen application can lead to nutrient runoff, harming nearby soil and water sources.
Legume crops such as soybean naturally fix nitrogen in the soil, improving fertility for future planting.
Balanced nitrogen management, along with phosphorus and potassium, is essential for sustainable crop production.
