Pesticides include a broad category of chemicals used to control pests, weeds, and diseases affecting crops.
Farmers rely on pesticide application to protect crop yield from insect, fungal, and weed damage.
Proper pesticide dosage and timing are essential to avoid harming beneficial organisms and the environment.
Integrated pest management encourages farmers to combine pesticide use with natural and biological control methods.
Pesticide residue on crops must be carefully managed to ensure food safety for consumers.
Overreliance on pesticide can lead to resistant pest populations, requiring stronger chemical interventions over time.
Sustainable pesticide practices balance effective pest control with long-term environmental and farmland health.
