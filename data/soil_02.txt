Different crops require different soil types, ranging from sandy soil for root vegetables to clay soil for rice cultivation.
Soil pH plays an important role in nutrient availability for plants and overall agricultural productivity.
Farmers use crop rotation and organic fertilizer to restore soil fertility after continuous cultivation.
Proper drainage systems prevent waterlogging, which can damage plant roots and reduce soil oxygen levels.
Soil compaction caused by heavy machinery can restrict root growth and reduce water absorption in farmland.
Adding organic matter such as compost improves soil structure, water retention, and microbial activity.
Sustainable soil management practices help farmers maintain productivity while protecting the environment for future generations.
