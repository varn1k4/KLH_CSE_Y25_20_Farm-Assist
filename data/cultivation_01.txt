Cultivation refers to the process of preparing land and growing crops through proper farming techniques.
Successful cultivation depends on factors such as soil fertility, water availability, and suitable climate conditions.
Farmers prepare the soil through ploughing and tilling before planting seeds for cultivation.
Proper spacing during cultivation ensures that plants receive adequate sunlight, water, and nutrients.
Crop rotation is an important cultivation practice that helps maintain soil fertility over multiple seasons.
Modern cultivation techniques combine traditional knowledge with technology to improve crop productivity.
Sustainable cultivation practices protect farmland while ensuring long-term agricultural production.
