Harvesting is the final stage of crop production where mature crops are collected from the field.
Timely harvesting is essential for maintaining crop quality, yield, and market value.
Farmers determine the right harvesting time based on grain color, moisture content, and plant maturity.
Delayed harvesting can lead to yield loss due to pest damage, disease, or adverse weather conditions.
Proper harvesting techniques help minimize crop damage and preserve nutritional quality.
After harvesting, crops are often dried and stored properly to prevent spoilage and pest infestation.
Efficient harvesting practices contribute significantly to overall farm productivity and profitability.
