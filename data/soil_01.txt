Soil is the foundation of all agricultural production, providing plants with water, nutrients, and physical support.
Soil fertility depends on organic matter content, nutrient balance, and proper moisture retention.
Farmers test soil regularly to determine nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium levels before applying fertilizer.
Healthy soil structure allows better root growth, water infiltration, and drainage for crops.
Organic compost and mulching are effective ways to improve soil fertility and long-term productivity.
Soil erosion caused by heavy rainfall or wind can significantly reduce farmland productivity over time.
Maintaining good soil health is essential for sustainable farming and consistent crop yield.
