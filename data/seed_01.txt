Seeds are the starting point of all crop production, and their quality directly affects crop yield.
Farmers select seeds based on factors such as disease resistance, climate suitability, and productivity potential.
Proper seed storage prevents damage from pests, moisture, and temperature fluctuations before planting.
High-quality seeds germinate faster and produce healthier plants with stronger root and stem development.
Seed treatment with fungicide can protect young plants from early disease infection.
Farmers often test seed germination rates before planting to ensure a good crop stand.
Improved seed varieties have played a major role in increasing agricultural productivity worldwide.
