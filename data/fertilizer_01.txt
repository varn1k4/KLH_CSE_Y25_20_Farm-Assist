Fertilizers provide essential nutrients required for healthy plant growth and improved crop productivity.
The three primary nutrients found in most fertilizers are nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium.
Nitrogen promotes leaf and stem growth, while phosphorus supports root development and potassium improves overall plant health.
Farmers apply fertilizer at different stages of crop growth to maximize yield and quality.
Organic fertilizers such as compost improve soil fertility naturally without harming the environment.
Excessive use of chemical fertilizer can lead to soil degradation and water pollution over time.
Balanced fertilizer application, combined with proper irrigation, is key to sustainable agricultural production.
