Different crops require different cultivation methods depending on soil type, climate, and water availability.
Farmers use irrigation and fertilizer management as part of standard cultivation practices to boost yield.
Weed control during cultivation prevents unwanted plants from competing with crops for nutrients and sunlight.
Organic cultivation methods rely on compost and natural fertilizer instead of chemical inputs.
Proper timing of cultivation activities such as planting and harvesting significantly affects crop quality.
Farmers who follow scientific cultivation practices often achieve higher productivity and better resource efficiency.
Cultivation practices must adapt to changing climate patterns to ensure consistent agricultural production.
