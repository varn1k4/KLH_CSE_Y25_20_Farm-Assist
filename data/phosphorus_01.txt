Phosphorus is a vital nutrient that supports root development, flowering, and fruit formation in crops.
Farmers apply phosphorus-rich fertilizer especially during the early stages of plant and root growth.
Phosphorus deficiency can result in poor root systems, delayed maturity, and reduced crop yield.
Soil fertility testing helps farmers determine the correct phosphorus levels needed for healthy crop growth.
Phosphorus works alongside nitrogen and potassium to support overall plant health and productivity.
Proper phosphorus management is especially important for flowering crops such as tomato and groundnut.
Long-term soil fertility depends on maintaining balanced phosphorus levels through organic or chemical fertilizer.
