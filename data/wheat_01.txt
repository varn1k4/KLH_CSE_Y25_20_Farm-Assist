Wheat is a major cereal crop cultivated widely across temperate agricultural regions.
It requires cooler temperature during the growing season and a dry period at the time of harvesting.
Soil fertility, adequate rainfall, and proper irrigation all contribute to a healthy wheat crop.
Farmers plant wheat seeds in well-prepared soil to encourage strong root and stem development.
Nutrient management with nitrogen and phosphorus fertilizers helps improve wheat grain quality and productivity.
Pest problems such as aphids and diseases like rust can lower wheat yield if left untreated.
Weeds growing among wheat plants reduce nutrient availability and must be removed through proper weed management.
