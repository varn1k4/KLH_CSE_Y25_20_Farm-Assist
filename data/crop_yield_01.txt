Crop yield refers to the amount of agricultural produce harvested from a given area of farmland.
Yield is influenced by factors such as soil fertility, water availability, seed quality, and climate conditions.
Farmers use fertilizer, irrigation, and pest management practices to maximize crop yield each season.
Disease-resistant seed varieties and proper crop rotation can significantly improve long-term yield stability.
Measuring crop yield helps farmers evaluate the effectiveness of their farming practices and make improvements.
Climate variability, including drought or excessive rainfall, can cause significant fluctuations in crop yield.
Consistent crop yield is essential for farmer income, food security, and overall agricultural sustainability.
