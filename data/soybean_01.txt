Soybean is an important legume crop grown for its protein-rich seeds used in food and animal feed production.
Soybean cultivation benefits from well-drained soil, warm temperature, and moderate rainfall conditions.
As a nitrogen-fixing crop, soybean can improve soil fertility naturally, reducing dependency on chemical fertilizer.
Pests such as aphids and diseases like rust can reduce soybean yield if not managed properly.
Proper irrigation during flowering and pod development stages is crucial for maximizing soybean productivity.
Weed control early in the growing season helps soybean plants establish healthy root systems.
Harvesting soybean at the correct maturity stage ensures optimal seed quality and yield.
