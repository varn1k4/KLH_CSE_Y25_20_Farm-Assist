Pests are one of the biggest threats to agricultural production, capable of destroying crops within days.
Common agricultural pests include insects such as aphids, stem borers, and whiteflies that damage plant leaves and stems.
Farmers use insecticides and pesticides to control pest populations and protect crop yield.
Integrated pest management combines biological, chemical, and cultural methods to reduce pest damage sustainably.
Regular field monitoring helps farmers detect early signs of pest infestation before it spreads across farmland.
Natural predators and beneficial insects can help control pest populations without excessive pesticide use.
Effective pest control is essential for maintaining healthy crops and achieving good agricultural productivity.
