Greenhouse farming allows farmers to grow crops in a controlled environment, protected from unfavorable climate conditions.
Temperature, sunlight, and humidity inside a greenhouse can be adjusted to optimize plant growth and yield.
Greenhouse cultivation is commonly used for high-value crops such as tomato and other vegetables.
Proper irrigation and fertilizer management remain essential even within controlled greenhouse farming systems.
Pest and disease control in greenhouses requires careful monitoring since enclosed spaces can favor rapid spread.
Greenhouse farming can extend the growing season and increase productivity regardless of outside climate conditions.
Investment in greenhouse infrastructure allows farmers to achieve more predictable and higher crop yields.
