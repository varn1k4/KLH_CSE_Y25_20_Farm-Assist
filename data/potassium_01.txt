Potassium is an essential nutrient that strengthens plant stems, improves disease resistance, and supports fruit quality.
Farmers apply potassium-rich fertilizer to crops such as banana, sugarcane, and potato to improve yield.
Potassium deficiency can cause weak stems, poor root development, and increased vulnerability to disease.
Balanced potassium levels help plants better withstand environmental stress such as drought and temperature extremes.
Soil testing helps farmers determine appropriate potassium fertilizer application for different crop types.
Potassium, together with nitrogen and phosphorus, forms the foundation of balanced crop nutrition programs.
Proper potassium management contributes significantly to overall farmland productivity and crop quality.
