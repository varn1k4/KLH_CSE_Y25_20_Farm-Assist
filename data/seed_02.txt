Seed selection is a critical decision that affects the entire crop cycle from planting to harvesting.
Hybrid seeds often provide better yield and disease resistance compared to traditional seed varieties.
Proper seed spacing during planting ensures adequate sunlight, water, and nutrients for each plant.
Farmers store seeds in cool, dry conditions to maintain their viability for the next planting season.
Seed quality directly influences root development, plant growth, and overall crop productivity.
Government agricultural programs often provide farmers with improved seed varieties to boost yield.
Investing in quality seeds is one of the most cost-effective ways to improve farm productivity.
