Modern harvesting methods use machinery to speed up the collection process for large-scale farming operations.
Manual harvesting is still common for delicate crops such as tomatoes and certain vegetables.
Farmers must consider weather conditions like rainfall before scheduling harvesting to avoid crop damage.
Post-harvest handling, including cleaning and storage, plays a key role in maintaining crop quality.
Harvesting at the correct growth stage ensures maximum yield and reduces post-harvest losses.
Proper harvesting equipment and techniques reduce grain damage and improve overall agricultural productivity.
Sustainable harvesting practices help farmers maintain soil health for the next planting season.
