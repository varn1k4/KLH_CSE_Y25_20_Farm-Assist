Effective water management is essential for maximizing crop yield while conserving limited water resources.
Farmers use irrigation scheduling based on soil moisture, rainfall, and crop water requirements.
Drip irrigation and sprinkler systems help reduce water wastage compared to traditional flood irrigation methods.
Proper drainage prevents waterlogging, which can damage roots and reduce soil fertility over time.
Water management practices must adapt to seasonal rainfall patterns and changing climate conditions.
Farmers who invest in efficient irrigation infrastructure often see improved crop productivity and resource use.
Balancing irrigation with natural rainfall is key to sustainable water management in agriculture.
