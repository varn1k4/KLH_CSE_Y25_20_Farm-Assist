Mango is a widely cultivated tropical fruit tree valued for its taste and nutritional benefits.
Mango cultivation thrives in warm climate with well-drained soil and moderate rainfall conditions.
Farmers apply balanced fertilizer to support flowering, fruit development, and overall tree productivity.
Pest problems such as fruit flies and diseases like anthracnose can significantly reduce mango yield and quality.
Proper irrigation during flowering and fruit development stages is essential for consistent mango production.
Pruning and weed control around mango trees help improve sunlight penetration and nutrient availability.
Harvesting mangoes at the right ripeness stage ensures better taste, quality, and market value.
