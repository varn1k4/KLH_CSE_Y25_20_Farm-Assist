Modern farm equipment such as tractors, tillers, and harvesters have significantly improved agricultural productivity.
Mechanized equipment allows farmers to prepare soil, plant seeds, and harvest crops more efficiently than manual labor.
Proper maintenance of farm equipment ensures reliable performance during critical planting and harvesting seasons.
Irrigation equipment, including pumps and sprinkler systems, plays a key role in water management for crops.
Farmers use specialized equipment for applying fertilizer and pesticide evenly across large farmland areas.
Investment in farm equipment can reduce labor costs while increasing overall farming efficiency and yield.
Access to affordable farm equipment remains a challenge for many smallholder farmers in developing regions.
