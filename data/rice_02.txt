Rice farming has evolved with better seed varieties, improved irrigation systems, and modern pest management techniques.
High-yield rice seeds combined with balanced fertilizer application can dramatically increase productivity per acre.
Weed control in the early growth stage is critical since weeds compete with young rice plants for nutrients and sunlight.
Rice diseases such as bacterial leaf blight and sheath rot spread quickly in humid climate conditions.
Farmers rely on fungicides and insecticides to protect rice crops from fungal infections and insect damage.
Good drainage after harvesting prevents waterlogging and helps maintain soil fertility for the next planting season.
Climate factors like rainfall pattern and temperature variation directly affect rice crop growth and final yield.
