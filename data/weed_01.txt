Weeds are unwanted plants that compete with crops for water, nutrients, sunlight, and space.
Uncontrolled weed growth can significantly reduce crop yield and overall farm productivity.
Farmers use manual weeding, mechanical tools, and herbicides to control weed populations in farmland.
Weeds often grow faster than crops, making early weed management essential for healthy crop growth.
Mulching is an effective method for suppressing weed growth while also conserving soil moisture.
Certain weeds can host pests and diseases that further threaten crop health and productivity.
Regular weed control throughout the growing season helps maintain soil fertility and crop quality.
