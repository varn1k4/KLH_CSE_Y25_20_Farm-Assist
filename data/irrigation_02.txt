Modern irrigation systems combine technology with traditional farming practices to optimize water usage.
Sprinkler irrigation is commonly used for crops like wheat and vegetables that require even water distribution.
Farmers must balance irrigation with drainage to prevent soil erosion and nutrient loss in farmland.
Water scarcity in many agricultural regions has led to increased adoption of efficient irrigation techniques.
Proper irrigation combined with fertilizer application supports healthy plant growth and higher crop productivity.
Seasonal rainfall patterns and temperature changes require farmers to adjust irrigation schedules throughout the year.
Investing in efficient irrigation infrastructure can help farmers cope with unpredictable climate and improve yield.
