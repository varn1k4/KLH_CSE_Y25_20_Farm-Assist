Potato is a widely cultivated root vegetable crop valued for its nutritional content and versatility.
Healthy potato growth requires well-drained soil, moderate temperature, and consistent water supply.
Farmers apply fertilizer containing phosphorus and potassium to support tuber development and yield.
Common pests such as potato beetles and diseases like blight can significantly damage potato crops.
Proper irrigation during tuber formation is essential for achieving good potato size and yield.
Weed control early in the growing season helps potato plants establish strong root systems.
Harvesting potatoes at the right maturity stage improves storage quality and reduces post-harvest losses.
