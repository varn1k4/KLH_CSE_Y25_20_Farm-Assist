Tomato cultivation practices have improved with the use of organic compost and mulching techniques.
Mulching helps retain soil moisture, suppress weed growth, and maintain a stable root temperature for tomato plants.
Farmers often stake or cage tomato plants to support stem growth and prevent fruit from touching the soil.
Balanced fertilizer containing nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium promotes healthy leaf and fruit development.
Diseases such as early blight and late blight spread rapidly in humid climate conditions, requiring quick fungicide treatment.
Proper drainage prevents root rot, which is a common problem in poorly drained tomato fields.
Crop rotation with tomato helps maintain soil fertility and reduces the buildup of pests and diseases over time.
