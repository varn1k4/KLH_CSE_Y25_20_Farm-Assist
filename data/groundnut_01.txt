Groundnut, also known as peanut, is an important oilseed and food crop grown in warm climate regions.
Groundnut cultivation requires well-drained sandy soil, adequate rainfall, and warm temperature for healthy growth.
Farmers apply fertilizer containing phosphorus and potassium to support pod development and improve yield.
Pest problems such as leaf miners and diseases like leaf spot can significantly affect groundnut productivity.
Proper irrigation is particularly important during the flowering and pod-filling stages of groundnut growth.
Weed management helps groundnut plants access adequate nutrients, water, and sunlight during early growth.
Harvesting groundnut at proper maturity ensures better pod quality and reduces post-harvest losses.
