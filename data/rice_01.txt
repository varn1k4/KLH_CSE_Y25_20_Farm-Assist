Rice is one of the most important cereal crops grown across the world, especially in Asia.
It is a staple food for more than half of the global population and forms the backbone of many farming economies.
Rice cultivation generally requires standing water, warm temperature, and fertile soil for healthy growth.
Farmers usually transplant rice seedlings into flooded fields known as paddies.
Proper irrigation is essential because rice plants are highly sensitive to water shortage during the flowering stage.
Nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium are the primary nutrients applied through fertilizer to boost rice yield.
Common pests such as stem borers and diseases like blast can significantly reduce rice production if not managed early.
Timely harvesting after the grain matures helps farmers preserve both quality and yield.
