Onion is a popular vegetable crop grown for its bulb, which is used widely in cooking around the world.
Onion cultivation requires well-drained soil, moderate rainfall, and adequate sunlight for healthy bulb development.
Farmers apply fertilizer with balanced nitrogen and potassium content to improve onion bulb size and quality.
Pest problems such as thrips and diseases like purple blotch can affect onion crop productivity.
Proper irrigation management is important during bulb formation to achieve optimal onion yield.
Weed control is critical in onion fields since onions have shallow roots and compete poorly with weeds.
Harvesting onions once the tops begin to dry ensures better storage life and market quality.
