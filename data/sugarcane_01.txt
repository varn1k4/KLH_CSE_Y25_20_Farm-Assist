Sugarcane is a tall, perennial crop cultivated primarily for sugar production and biofuel purposes.
Sugarcane requires warm temperature, high rainfall or irrigation, and fertile soil for optimal growth.
Farmers apply fertilizer rich in nitrogen and potassium to support healthy stem and sugar content development.
Pest and disease management, including control of borers and rust, is essential for maintaining sugarcane yield.
Proper irrigation is critical during the early growth stages when sugarcane requires consistent water supply.
Weed control in sugarcane fields prevents competition for nutrients and supports healthy crop development.
Harvesting sugarcane at peak maturity ensures maximum sugar content and agricultural productivity.
