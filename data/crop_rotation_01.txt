Crop rotation is the practice of growing different crops in sequence on the same land to maintain soil fertility.
Rotating crops such as legumes with cereals helps replenish nitrogen levels naturally in the soil.
Farmers use crop rotation to reduce the buildup of pests and diseases associated with continuous single-crop farming.
Proper crop rotation planning considers soil type, climate, and the nutrient needs of different crops.
Crop rotation can improve long-term farmland productivity while reducing dependency on chemical fertilizer.
Combining crop rotation with organic compost application further enhances soil fertility and structure.
Many sustainable farming systems rely heavily on crop rotation to maintain healthy, productive farmland.
