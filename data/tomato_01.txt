Tomato is a widely cultivated vegetable crop known for its nutritional value and market demand.
Healthy tomato growth depends on well-drained soil, adequate sunlight, and consistent water supply.
Farmers use irrigation systems such as drip irrigation to deliver water directly to tomato plant roots.
Nutrient-rich fertilizer application supports strong stem growth and better fruit production in tomato plants.
Common pests like whiteflies and diseases such as blight can severely damage tomato leaves and fruit.
Regular pest and disease monitoring helps farmers apply pesticides and fungicides before infestations spread.
Harvesting tomatoes at the right ripeness stage ensures better taste, shelf life, and market value.
