Healthy plant growth depends on a combination of soil fertility, water availability, sunlight, and temperature.
Root development in the early growth stage determines how effectively a plant absorbs water and nutrients.
Stem strength supports the plant structure and allows nutrients to travel efficiently to leaves and fruit.
Leaves play a vital role in photosynthesis, converting sunlight into energy for overall plant growth.
Farmers monitor plant growth stages closely to apply fertilizer, water, and pest control at the right time.
Environmental stress such as drought or extreme temperature can significantly slow down plant growth and yield.
Balanced nutrient management supports strong root, stem, and leaf development throughout the crop life cycle.
