Fungicides are substances used to control fungal diseases that affect crop leaves, stems, and roots.
Farmers apply fungicide preventively or after detecting early symptoms of fungal infection in crops.
Common fungal diseases such as blight and rust can spread rapidly in humid climate conditions.
Proper fungicide application timing is critical for effective disease control and crop protection.
Overuse of fungicide can lead to resistance in fungal pathogens, reducing long-term effectiveness.
Combining fungicide use with good field sanitation and crop rotation improves overall disease management.
Farmers should follow safety guidelines when handling and applying fungicide to protect health and environment.
