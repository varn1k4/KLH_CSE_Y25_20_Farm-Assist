Maize, also known as corn, is a widely cultivated cereal crop used for food, feed, and industrial purposes.
Maize requires warm temperature, adequate rainfall, and fertile soil for optimal growth and yield.
Farmers apply nitrogen-rich fertilizer during the early growth stages to support strong stem and leaf development.
Pest problems such as corn borers and diseases like leaf blight can significantly reduce maize productivity.
Proper irrigation during the flowering stage is critical for maximizing maize grain yield.
Weed control in young maize fields prevents competition for nutrients, water, and sunlight.
Harvesting maize at the right moisture level ensures better storage quality and reduces post-harvest losses.
