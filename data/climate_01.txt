Climate plays a crucial role in determining which crops can be successfully cultivated in a given region.
Temperature, rainfall, and sunlight are the primary climate factors affecting agricultural productivity.
Farmers must adapt their planting and harvesting schedules according to seasonal climate patterns.
Climate change has introduced unpredictable rainfall and temperature variations that challenge traditional farming practices.
Crops like rice require warm temperature and consistent water supply, while wheat prefers cooler climate conditions.
Extreme weather events such as drought or flooding can severely damage crops and reduce farm yield.
Understanding local climate patterns helps farmers make informed decisions about irrigation and fertilizer management.
