Modern agricultural technology has transformed traditional farming practices through automation and data-driven decision making.
Farmers increasingly use sensors and irrigation controllers to optimize water usage based on real-time soil moisture data.
Precision agriculture techniques help farmers apply fertilizer and pesticide more efficiently, reducing waste and environmental impact.
Drones and satellite imagery are used to monitor crop health, detect pest infestations, and estimate yield.
Agricultural technology also includes improved seed varieties developed through research to enhance disease resistance and productivity.
Farm management software helps farmers track planting schedules, irrigation needs, and harvesting timelines more effectively.
The adoption of agricultural technology continues to improve productivity while promoting more sustainable farming practices.
