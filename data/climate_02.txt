Adapting to changing climate conditions has become essential for sustainable agricultural production.
Farmers use climate-resistant seed varieties to cope with unpredictable rainfall and temperature changes.
Proper irrigation planning helps offset the effects of reduced rainfall during dry climate periods.
Rising temperature levels can accelerate pest and disease outbreaks, requiring more active crop management.
Farmers increasingly rely on weather forecasts to plan planting, irrigation, and harvesting activities.
Climate-smart agricultural practices combine traditional knowledge with modern technology to improve resilience.
Long-term climate planning is essential for maintaining consistent crop yield and farmland productivity.
