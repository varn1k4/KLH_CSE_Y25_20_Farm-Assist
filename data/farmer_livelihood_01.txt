Farmers form the backbone of agricultural production, dedicating significant time and resources to crop cultivation.
Access to quality seed, fertilizer, and irrigation infrastructure directly affects farmer productivity and income.
Many farmers face challenges such as unpredictable climate, pest outbreaks, and fluctuating market prices.
Government agricultural programs often provide farmers with subsidies, training, and improved seed varieties.
Sustainable farming practices help farmers maintain long-term soil fertility while ensuring consistent crop yield.
Farmer cooperatives and agricultural extension services play an important role in sharing knowledge and resources.
Supporting farmer livelihoods is essential for ensuring food security and stable agricultural production worldwide.
