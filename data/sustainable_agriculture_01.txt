Sustainable agriculture focuses on farming practices that maintain productivity while protecting environmental resources for future generations.
Crop rotation, organic fertilizer, and efficient irrigation are key components of sustainable agricultural systems.
Farmers practicing sustainable agriculture aim to preserve soil fertility, conserve water, and reduce chemical dependency.
Integrated pest management supports sustainable agriculture by minimizing pesticide use while controlling pest populations effectively.
Sustainable farming practices help reduce soil erosion, protect biodiversity, and maintain long-term farmland productivity.
Many farmers are adopting sustainable agriculture methods in response to growing environmental and climate concerns.
Sustainable agriculture ultimately supports both farmer livelihoods and the long-term health of agricultural ecosystems.
