Pest outbreaks often increase during favorable climate conditions such as warm temperature and high humidity.
Different crops attract different types of pests, requiring farmers to use crop-specific pest control strategies.
Pesticides must be applied carefully to avoid harming beneficial insects and contaminating soil or water sources.
Crop rotation is an effective method for reducing pest buildup and improving long-term farmland health.
Farmers who ignore early pest signs risk significant yield loss and reduced crop quality at harvest time.
Biological pest control using natural predators is gaining popularity as a sustainable alternative to chemical pesticides.
Proper pest management protects both crop yield and the overall health of the farming ecosystem.
