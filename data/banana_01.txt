Banana is a popular tropical fruit crop cultivated widely for its nutritional and economic value.
Healthy banana growth requires warm temperature, high rainfall or irrigation, and fertile, well-drained soil.
Farmers apply fertilizer rich in potassium to support fruit development and improve banana yield and quality.
Pest problems such as banana weevils and diseases like Panama wilt can severely affect crop productivity.
Consistent irrigation is essential for banana plants due to their high water requirement throughout growth.
Weed control around banana plants helps maintain soil moisture and reduces competition for nutrients.
Harvesting bananas at the right maturity stage ensures better quality and extends shelf life during transport.
