Different crops require different fertilizer combinations depending on soil fertility and growth stage.
Farmers often conduct soil testing before applying fertilizer to determine nutrient deficiencies in farmland.
Nitrogen-based fertilizers are commonly used for leafy crops, while phosphorus supports flowering and fruiting stages.
Potassium-rich fertilizers help improve crop resistance to disease and environmental stress such as drought.
Organic compost and manure serve as natural fertilizer alternatives that improve long-term soil health.
Proper fertilizer management reduces the risk of nutrient runoff, which can contaminate nearby water sources.
Sustainable fertilizer practices support both high crop yield and long-term farmland productivity.
